Where the loop runs

Built in the hospital. Works wherever teams train.

The first prototypes were built and tested with clinical teams, which is why our examples come from the hospital. The method itself is not medical: it works in any discipline where experts train a team that has to operate together.

Healthcare

Team coordination and patient–caregiver interaction in clinical interventions and simulations.

Record a simulation → debrief → run the scenario again

Business

Line management, negotiation, sales and recruitment.

Record a negotiation → review dynamics → practise again

Education

Immersive learning and interprofessional simulation.

Record a class exercise → objective feedback → improve
